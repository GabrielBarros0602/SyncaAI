What this screen decides

  • Where the request starts: one “Plan” button in the header, the same place at every width, next to Menu. On the phone, the bottom bar belongs to moving between days and the header is the one thing always on screen, so Plan goes up there. Plan is a destination, not a quick capture, and the button is also where the plan reports back: its label carries the state.
  • Where the wait shows: on that button, as a word (“Plan · queued”, “Plan · making…”, “Plan · ready”). Nothing moves, so reduced motion changes nothing. When the plan is ready, the status region says so above the day bar, with Review, and it stays until it’s dismissed or acted on, since a ready plan isn’t something to time out. The request lives on the server, so after a reload the button shows the same state. Here that’s stood in for by local storage.
  • The review on the phone: a full view that replaces the day (‹ Week to go back). Steps are rows like tasks: at rest they show the time or the reason they have none, the length, “after step N”, the description and the links, and no verbs. Tapping opens Edit and Remove. Approve and Discard take the place of the day bar while the review is open, and above them a line says how many steps have no time yet, before the server has to. “What it adds” shows each day as booked → booked with the plan, out of the budget.
  • The review at 1100 and up: a 440px panel beside the week. Proposed steps sit in their days with a dashed edge, “proposed”, in ink-2, so you can see where they land while you review. From 640 to 1099 it’s the phone’s view at a wider measure (see 768).
  • Progress: on the Plan button (“Plan · 3 of 5”), and as the large figure on the plan’s page, “3 of 5 preparations done”. Each task that came from the plan says so with a link back (“from your plan · step 2”), so you can get to the count from anywhere a step shows. A deleted task leaves the total, and the page says that it did.
  • The accent marks refusals, failed requests, the approve report and a step the server named. Not this week, no room and after another step aren’t errors; they’re a reason and an action, in neutral ink.
  • Writing by hand is the same draft, empty, with a “What it’s for” field in place of the request. It’s always next to “Make a plan”, and it’s the action offered when the model is down or planning is paused.
  • Numbers: the usable day is 07:00–23:00, Saturday and Sunday are days off, the budget is 16h and the block is 60 minutes. This week, Thursday has three 30-minute gaps after 14:25 and Friday is already over budget, so three steps fit and three wait for next week, Monday from 07:00.
